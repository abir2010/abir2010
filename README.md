### 📜 Selected Research & Contributions
* **Spatio-Temporal Generative Modeling (Undergraduate Thesis):** Engineered the first learned gloss-to-pose generation system for Bangladeshi Sign Language (BdSL)[cite: 78]. Conducted a rigorous empirical evaluation of denoising diffusion, autoregressive, and non-autoregressive architectures[cite: 78]. While I developed a diffusion pipeline with a custom velocity penalty to mitigate Soft-DTW temporal collapse[cite: 78, 120, 121], our findings demonstrated that a non-autoregressive regression model (NAT + MSE) combined with a BanglaBERT encoder and explicit duration modeling achieved superior geometric accuracy (99.1% PCK@0.5) and inference speed[cite: 78]. Additionally introduced a leak-free dynamic-time-warping evaluation protocol for template-structured sign corpora[cite: 78].
* **Graph Learning & Medical AI (Publications):** Co-authored two research papers accepted and presented at **IEEE QPAIN 2026**, focusing on Federated Graph-transformers and Hypergraph Contrastive Learning. 
* **Data Engineering & Curation:** Independently curated and published *Shongbad Chitro*, a comprehensive 50,000-sample multimodal dataset designed to facilitate regional vision-language modeling and text-image alignment research.
